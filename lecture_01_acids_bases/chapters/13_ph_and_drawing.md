# Thermal Paper

When the print head applies heat (around 100°C), a chemical called a "sensitizer" melts. 
This creates a localized liquid pool. 
The acid and the dye dissolve into this pool, finally touch, and the acid donates its $H^+$ (proton) to the leuco dye.
This proton transfer forces the lactone ring of the dye to snap open, changing its molecular structure from colorless to black. 
When the print head moves away, the pool instantly re-solidifies, locking the dye in its colored state.
