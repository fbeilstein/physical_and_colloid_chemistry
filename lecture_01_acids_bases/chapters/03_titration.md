# Polyprotic Acid Titration Curve

:::matrix{cols="50/50"}
[[0,0]]
$$
\ce{H3PO3(aq) <=> H2PO3-(aq) + H+(aq)}
$$
$$
K_{a1} = \frac{[\ce{H2PO3-}][\ce{H+}]}{\ce{H3PO3}}
$$
$$
\ce{H2PO3-(aq) <=> HPO3^{2-}(aq) + H+(aq)}
$$
$$
K_{a2} = \frac{[\ce{HPO3^{2-}}][\ce{H+}]}{\ce{H2PO3-}}
$$
