# Maillard Browning

:::matrix{cols="50/50"}
[[0,0]]
The Maillard reaction is a chemical reaction between amino acids and reducing sugars to create melanoidins, the compounds that give browned food its distinctive flavor.
[[0,1]]
![](./lecture_01_acids_bases/images/slide_64_img_109.png)
[[1,0]]
Nucleophilic attack as the first step (we will consider mechanisms later).

Note that alkaline conditions favour $\ce{NH2}$ not $\ce{NH3+}$, thus making proteins better nucleophiles and enhance Maillard browning

:::
